Open & eligible — the plain-language shortlist

Drugs approved overseas that have never been approved in the US (so they qualify for the congressional voucher program) and look freely available to develop — sorted with the likely off-patent ones first. Each still needs a patent / rights check before you commit; those are marked.

⚠️ Read this first. "Off-patent" here is inferred from how long ago the drug was approved abroad — a signal, not a legal clearance. And the older a drug is, the less certain the "never US-approved" test is: the underlying US list is NDA-based, so a drug once approved in the US and later withdrawn (e.g. an old anesthetic or a discontinued product) can wrongly appear here as eligible. For any old candidate, re-confirm both eligibility (was it ever US-approved, including withdrawn/pre-1938?) and freedom-to-operate before acting.
